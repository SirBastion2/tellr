# A-03 · Latency benchmark

**Agent A · hour 6, at most 30 minutes · **checkpoint CP1**** · depends on A-02 merged

**Read only:** architecture §8.1–§8.3

## Goal

A *measured* p95 so the drivers can choose the model. This is not the `LocalLLM`; that comes in A-05.

## You may create or edit

- `scripts/bench_llm.py`

## Steps (tests first)

1. Start `llama-server` in tmux with a 3B Q4_K_M model; if VRAM allows, repeat with an 8B Q4_K_M.
2. Build a representative prompt (a stable prefix of about 1,000 tokens, a suffix of about 250 built from the library and T2's transcript), and a 12-way GBNF grammar enumerating question IDs (§8.2). Temperature 0.
3. Warm the prefix cache, then time 50 runs: total, prefill, and decode, at p50 and p95. Record the GPU and its VRAM.
4. Compare with L1b (p95 at most 1.5 s) and the §8.3 target (about 0.7 s).

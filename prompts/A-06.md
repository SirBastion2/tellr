## Goal

Three arms, honest metrics, reproducible runs.

## You may create or edit

- `eval/**`

## Steps (tests first)

1. **First commit, before any run:** `eval/PREREGISTRATION.md` with arm A's questions, its per-question chip-to-concerning mapping (polarity, §13.2), arm A's threshold, and the metric definitions. Commit it on its own. The commit order is the evidence it came first.
2. `eval/validate.py`: checks scenario files use valid intents, chips, purposes, and context chips. It prints no scores; B-07 relies on that.
3. 30 development scenarios in `eval/scenarios/dev/`: 15 coached (at least 4 cover stories whose stated purpose differs from the truth, at least 3 perfectly coached) and 15 legitimate (at least 4 with innocent red-flag chips, at least 3 vague but genuine). Seed from T1–T7. Base the rest on public scam guidance (draft §15).
4. **Tests first:** the same scenario run twice gives identical results; arm B on T1–T7 reproduces the golden traces.
5. `eval/run.py`: arms A, B, C; batch size 1, no prefetch, no timeout, temperature 0. Writes `eval/results/<git-sha>/results.json` and `report.md`: the confusion matrix on final raw level, headline counts, friction rate, misses listed by name, questions to decision, `default_fired_rate`, validator rejections, trivial baselines, Wilson intervals.
